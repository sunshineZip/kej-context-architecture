## Document Purpose

Scope and load conditions for the Hopp-slægten domain — the family line descending from Knud Danielsen Hopp (b. c.1640, d.1723, herredsfoged i Salling Nørre Herred), the confirmed root of this project's own direct-line research. This is KEJ's own line, reaching him through generations not yet fully documented — see `knowledge.md` §2 for exactly how far the confirmed chain currently reaches.

## Domain Scope

**This domain covers:**
- Knud Danielsen Hopp (b. c.1640) and his confirmed descendants, per KEJ's own "Hopp-slægten" chapter series — chapters 01, 01C, 01F, 02, 03, 04 fully read; chapters 05-16 not yet received.
- The confirmed direct line toward KEJ (generations 01-05), and the point past which it becomes unconfirmed (generation 06 onward — seven candidate children, none confirmed as the actual continuation).
- Two working hypotheses about generations *before* Knud Danielsen Hopp (pre-1640), developed independently of KEJ's own material.
- External secondary sources bearing on this line (an outside genealogist's page; an unrelated third party's ancestor chart that happens to share this same root ancestor).

**This domain does NOT cover:**
- The Boe-slægten line — a separate domain (`knowledge/domains/boe-slaegten/`). The two meet only through Bruno Dølby marrying into the Boe line in 2000.
- Anything in `restricted/` — this domain never restates restricted content; it points to it.

**Overlap risk:** None currently beyond the Bruno Dølby link already noted in the Boe-slægten domain.

## Working Constraints

- **`family-tree/tree.ged` is authoritative** (IDs `@I364@`-`@I757@` roughly) — this domain synthesizes and explains, it doesn't duplicate.
- **The direct line is confirmed only through generation 05** (Nicolai Danielsen Hopp, b.1780) — everything past that point, including which of generation 06's several children actually continues to KEJ, is unconfirmed. Never state or imply the line continues further than `knowledge.md` §2 documents as confirmed.
- **The "our line" depth policy governs how side branches are modeled**: full biographical depth for the confirmed direct line; children and grandchildren only for side branches; deeper generations capped once they clearly diverge, especially once they reach likely-living individuals. See `knowledge.md` §4.
- **Two pre-1640 hypotheses exist, developed independently of KEJ's own material** — treat them as exactly that, not as findings. Full write-up: `projects/archive-digitization/context/hopp-slaegten-pre-1640-hypothesis.md`.
- **KEJ is the final authority on genealogical conclusions**, per the standing `grandfather-review/` Hard Constraint.
- **GPS/Evidence Explained citation standard applies** (`authoring-guidelines.md` §9.5).
