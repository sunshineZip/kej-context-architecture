## 1. Executive Summary

- Confirmed root: **Knud Danielsen Hopp** (b. c.1640, d.1723) — herredsfoged i Salling Nørre Herred from 1673, herredsskriver from 1688, presided over the 1686 "Tise Trolde" witch trial (§3).
- The **confirmed direct line reaches only to generation 05** (Nicolai Danielsen Hopp, b.1780) — past that, generation 06 had at least seven children, none confirmed as the actual continuation toward KEJ. See §2 for the exact shape of this gap — it is more specific than "chapters not yet received."
- Six chapters fully read (01, 01C, 01F, 02, 03, 04); chapters 05-16 not yet received. 394 individuals in `family-tree/tree.ged` for this line (`@I364@`-`@I757@`).
- 01C alone — one side branch — produced 230 individuals across one single further sub-branch, prompting the "our line" depth policy (§4).
- Two working hypotheses exist about generations *before* 1640, developed independently of KEJ's own material, plus a third that was raised and discarded — see §5.
- A completely unrelated third party (a Danish physician and reform activist, Dr. Jonathan Høegh Leunbach) shares this exact root ancestor via a different child — see §6.
- Two specific content gaps (missing maps/biography in chapters 01 and 01F) are tracked as `grandfather-review/queue.md` items 18-19.

## 3. Notable History

**The 1686 "Tise Trolde" witch trial.** As herredsfoged, Knud Danielsen Hopp presided over the trial of two sisters, Anne and Mette Madsdatter, who confessed (willingly, per the source) to witchcraft. The trial was brief — witnesses heard 6.7.1686, verdict the same day, confirmed on appeal 28.7 — and the sisters were burned at Breum Hede that year, despite a royal order just one week earlier (21.7.1686) that no witch be burned without a Supreme Court ruling. [VERIFIED: hopp-slaegten, 01-oversigt / external-source-hopp-herredsfoged.md]

**Mogens Knudsen Hopp's counterfeiting scandal (01F, branch "a.").** Became sognedegn i Jebjerg-Lyby in 1758 by marrying into the post. In 1765, accused with an itinerant stranger of manufacturing counterfeit coins; both fled before officials arrived, leaving an estate worth almost nothing. A manhunt followed with a circulated physical description noting he'd fled with his 11-year-old son. A destitute man died in Rennerlund in July 1765, accompanied by a boy giving the name "Peder" — close enough to the fugitive's description that the court accepted it as proof of death without formal identification. The 1767 Landsting verdict found him guilty in absentia. [VERIFIED: hopp-slaegten, 01F]

**A Viborg dyer-family saga (01F, branch "d.").** Dorthe Cathrine Danielsdatter Hopp had an illegitimate son who became, in the source's own words, "stamfar til adskillige borgere i Viborg." She then married two successive Viborg farvere (dyers): Laurits Schorup, who bought the Viborg farvergård in 1805 for 4,900 rigsdaler, and — after his 1807 death — Detlef Jessen, who sold the same property in 1812 for 26,000 rigsdaler and later oversaw dyeing labor by inmates at the Viborg tugthus. [VERIFIED: hopp-slaegten, 01F]

**A drunk-but-competent schoolteacher (01F, branch "d.").** Knud Danielsen Hopp (a nephew, not the root ancestor — a namesake, `@I712@`) became degn/skolelærer in Strandby (1811-1849). Oral-history accounts collected c.1890 by folklorist Evald Tang Kristensen preserve his own reported response when found stuck in a creek on the way home — but his own neat, legible handwriting survives in three census returns he personally recorded, suggesting the drinking never visibly compromised his actual work. [VERIFIED: hopp-slaegten, 01F]

**Two brothers, identical names (chapter 04, branch "d.").** A genuine, confirmed case, not a transcription artifact: two full brothers, born 1812 and 1814, both named "Anders Frederichsen Hop." The source treats them as two distinct people throughout, with entirely separate marriages and lives — one became a smith and later a husmand/høker near Ribe; the other stayed near Vedersø as a husmand and day-laborer. Both are modeled as separate individuals in `tree.ged`. [VERIFIED: hopp-slaegten, 04]

## 4. Side Branches and the "Our Line" Depth Policy

The chapter numbering (01→02→...→07, following whichever child the source itself identifies as continuing toward KEJ) shows early on that 01C and 01F are side branches, not the direct chain — full depth is reserved for the confirmed direct-line chapters (02 onward), while side branches get children and grandchildren of the file's own subject modeled, with deeper generations summarized or capped, especially once they clearly reach likely-living individuals.

**01C is the largest single body of material in this domain** — Niels Jørgen Knudsen Hopp's own line. One single sub-branch (his granddaughter Dorthe Nielsdatter Hopp's own child "I.," Anne Cathrine Nicolaisdatter Hjardemaal — the jordemoder/midwife in Viborg) alone produced 230 individuals and 75 families, read to full depth at the human's explicit request before the "our line" policy was adopted. This is the same Hjardemaal line that turns out to connect to Dr. Jonathan Høegh Leunbach — see §6.

01F's six children (a.-f.) are all short, closed, or capped per the policy — see §3 for the two most notable (Mogens's scandal, the Viborg dyer family). Chapter 04's six children (a.-f.) are all read to at least two generations per the cap, including the same-name-siblings case (§3).

## 5. Pre-1640 Hypotheses

Full write-up: `projects/archive-digitization/context/hopp-slaegten-pre-1640-hypothesis.md`. Summary:

- **Hypothesis 1 (active):** Mads Hop, arriving in Skagen c.1625, is the common ancestor behind *multiple* documented Hop/Hopp branches in North/Central Jutland — not just this family's line (via Daniel Madsen Hopp), but also a second, separately-documented branch via another son, Hans Madsen Hop. Both traced to the same uncited source, so not independent corroboration, but a coherent single-founder narrative if the source is accurate.
- **Hypothesis A (active):** Daniel Madsen Hopp's birth year, given as 1633 in one source, is a recording error — a 7-year-old cannot father the son (Knud Danielsen Hopp, b.1640) attributed to him. A different source's own summary chart already silently corrects this to "omk 1620" without citation. Candidate resolution: primary kirkebog records, logged in `research-queue.md`.
- **Hypothesis B (discarded 2026-08-09):** that Knud Danielsen Hopp might be Mads Hop's direct son rather than grandson. Discarded on patronymic-naming grounds — "Danielsen" specifically requires a father named Daniel, confirmed independently across `tree.ged`, and both external sources. Kept in the source file, marked discarded, not deleted.

Neither active hypothesis has been shown to KEJ yet — see `grandfather-review/queue.md` item 2.

## 6. External Sources and a Shared-Ancestor Discovery

Two external secondary sources bear on this line, both preserved in full in `projects/archive-digitization/context/`:

- **`external-source-hopp-herredsfoged.md`** (akj-cbj.dk, Arne Bjørn Jørgensen) — a different family researcher's page, whose primary source for Knud Danielsen Hopp's own biography is explicitly KEJ's own published 1978/1991 articles. Confirms generations 01 and 01F's death years exactly; adds the pre-1640 chain (Hans Hop → Mads Hopp → Daniel Madsen Hopp) as an uncited hypothesis (§5).
- **`external-source-leunbach-anetavle.md`** (Svend Aage Nielsen, 2021) — an ancestor chart for a completely unrelated third party, Dr. Jonathan Høegh Leunbach (1884-1955, a Danish physician and sexual-health/abortion-rights reformer), found by chance via a personal web search. His ancestor #196 is the *same* Knud Danielsen Hopp — confirmed via exact name/date/spouse match against `tree.ged#I364` — reached via a different child (Niels Jørgen Knudsen Hopp → Dorthe Nielsdatter Hopp → the Hjardemaal family, §4) than this family's own direct line. Also names two previously-unconfirmed children of Knud Danielsen Hopp (Karen and Helle Knudsdatter Hopp) and extends the Hjardemaal line one generation past what `tree.ged` currently models. Its own source list for Knud Danielsen Hopp's biography also names KEJ directly — likely the same underlying research resurfacing through a different compiler, not independent confirmation. See `grandfather-review/queue.md` item 20.
