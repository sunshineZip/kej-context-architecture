## Checkpoint 1 — Daniel Nielsen Hopp himself + branch "a." (2026-08-09)

Daniel Nielsen Hopp succeeded his father Niels directly as degn in Vedersø in 1775, serving until his own death on 1 January 1800. Like his father, he'd spent his younger years administering probate for the neighboring Åberg estate. Married twice: first Mette Nielsdatter Bjerg (1776, from Bøvling), who died in 1793 leaving 5 minor children — his own letter to the provost, quoted directly in the source, is a small, plain piece of grief: *"Jeg lader dem vide, at min Konne er død, og det fra 5 umyndige Børn."* The probate that followed is fully transcribed, down to individual household items and their values, closing with barely 23 rigsdaler left after debts. He remarried Edel Nielsdatter Hug, a widow, who outlived him and remarried again after his own 1800 death.

**A source-internal date error worth flagging and cross-checking rather than silently trusting:** Edel's own death date is given in this chapter as "8.7.1910" — clearly impossible on its face (she was born 1744). Chapter 03's own Kilder list, read the previous checkpoint, independently gives "Edele Hopp begr. 8.7.1810" for the same event — confirming 1810 is correct and this chapter's "1910" is a transcription/OCR slip. Resolved using the cross-reference rather than guessing.

**All 6 children of the 1st marriage overviewed**, per the standing "our line" cap for children of the file's direct-line subject:

- **a. Niels Danielsen Hopp (Hoppe)** (`#I722`) — became a hattemager (hatter) in Viborg, then Skive; a newspaper item from 1804 (quoted in full) credits him with saving a drowning man by fetching lanterns. Ended his life as a registered pauper in Skive, dying of what the source guesses was gout. **His own 6 children (grandchildren of the chapter's subject) modeled in full per the cap's own two-generation rule** — one (Anders Daniel) became a Sjælland forester with a family tradition of noble ancestry the source treats skeptically; another (Christian Nielsen Hopp) became a master hatter, guild founder, and eventually also a glue manufacturer in Viborg. Two (Peder, Daniel) have almost nothing recorded beyond a birth date. One (Frederik) became a forester near Slagelse whose own son fathered an unusually well-documented multi-generation line of foresters, itself capped here (documented well into the 1920s, clearly beyond the two-generation line).
- **b. Karen Christence Danielsdatter Hopp** — per the overview, died before 1786; not yet independently re-confirmed against her own short detailed entry (next checkpoint).
- **c. Nicolai Danielsen Hopp** — the direct-line continuation to chapter 05, not yet received; only vitals already in hand from the overview table are retained (`#I373`, unchanged this checkpoint).
- **d.-f.** — not yet read; deferred to the next checkpoint(s).
