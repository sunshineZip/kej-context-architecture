**Citation and fidelity methodology (added v1.2):** every claim below now carries a `[VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.N]` or `p.N-M` tag, not just the one file-level citation this document opened with — a real gap when this eventually promotes into a domain, since re-attaching citations fact-by-fact after the fact is worse than doing it as each section is read. Two practices, already partly in use and now made explicit: (1) quote consequential atomic facts — dates, ages, named individuals, and any passage that reads as a direct kirkebog/skifte transcription — close to verbatim rather than paraphrasing them, so a mismatch is visible at a glance; (2) precise per-claim citations (this paragraph) make it a cheap, localized check for KEJ or the human to compare a specific claim against the original document, rather than needing a full independent re-transcription pass, which would cost roughly as much as the original extraction for uncertain benefit given it would be the same reader checking their own reading.

## 02. Peder Christensen Boe (o.1731–1810) — Detailed Notes

**Vital facts:** born o.1731 (place not stated); died 17.8.1810, Sdr. Dråby, age 79. Husmand (Jørsby, from 1775, tenant under Nygård estate) → fæstegårdmand (Sdr. Dråby, from ~1785, under Ullerup estate). Trolovet 29.12.1775; married (viet) 16.2.1776, Jørsby kirke, to Sidsel (Zidsel) Lauridsdatter Toft. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Fadder (godparent) appearances cited from Jørsby kirkebog:** 1771 (Mads Andersen "på Heden" & Margrethe Josephsdatter's son Anders, b.8.5); 1772 (Jens Sørensen Heede & Maren Larsdatter's daughter Maren, b.9.10 — together with future wife Zidsel); 1773 (Zidsel's own "illegitimate" son Mads, b.8.5 — see conflict below); 1774 and 1785 (Christen Larsen Toft & Inger Michelsdatter's son Michel, b.16.3). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Wife — Zidsel (Sidsel) Lauridsdatter Toft:** born o.1747, Jørsby, daughter of Laurids Nielsen Toft (d.1797). Confirmed 1765, age 18. Died 25.12.1821, Sdr. Dråby, age 66 (matches Aneoversigt). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**1787 folketælling** (Sdr. Dråby): Peder Christensen Boe, 60 [age doesn't reconcile cleanly with b. o.1731 → would be ~56; not resolved here], bonde/gårdbeboer; wife Zidsel Laursdatter, 38; 3 joint children (a–c, below); plus Zidsel's son Mads, 14, "hendes Søn avlet uden Ægteskab" (her son begotten outside marriage). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Notable documented history — the "Kyssesekten" (Kiss Sect):** A religious sect active in Jørsby led by Peder Lassen Grud, based on a literal reading of "greet one another with a holy kiss" (Paul's epistles). Peder Lassen fathered children by at least 4 women, was prosecuted, sentenced to labor at the Copenhagen fortress and estate forfeiture (later reduced on appeal), served ~2 years, then returned and continued the sect for some years. Zidsel Lauridsdatter Toft (later wife of Peder Christensen Boe, 02) was one of the women involved: her son Mads Pedersen was fathered by Peder Lassen out of wedlock — the manuscript quotes what reads like a direct kirkebog record naming Peder Lassen as declared father ("blef udlagt til Barne Fader"). Peder Boe himself fell away from the sect in 1773 and testified against Peder Lassen. Manuscript cites "Historisk Årbog for Thy og Mors" 1968-70 and Steen Busck's "En alternativ bevægelse i 1700-årene" as secondary sources for this episode. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Mads Pedersen (Boe)** — son of Zidsel Lauridsdatter Toft and Peder Lassen Grud (per the above), later took the name "Boe" after his stepfather Peder Christensen Boe. Married Karen Pedersdatter Krøll (banns 1799, Sdr. Dråby); took over stepfather's farm 1802; later remarried Karen Christensdatter (by 1834); died 10.11.1847, Sdr. Dråby, aftægtsmand, age 76. Descendants still carried the Boe name as of 2019 per the manuscript. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8] — birth year deliberately omitted here, see Open Items #1 and `grandfather-review/queue.md` #4.

**Boe surname origin — explicitly stated as unknown by KEJ himself:** the manuscript states plainly that the origin of the "Boe" name on Mors is not known, and explicitly considers and rejects (tentatively) a connection to a separate "Sallingboe" family in Sønder Dråby despite the name similarity, for lack of any documented common link. Worth noting this is KEJ's own methodological rigor already in evidence — he flags his own uncertainty rather than asserting a tidy origin story. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Children of 02 and Sidsel, b. 1777–1784, Jørsby (labeled a–c):**
- **a. Anna Pedersdatter Boe**, b. 29.3.1777, Jørsby. Faddere: Laurs Ammidsbøl, Michel Laursen, Ole Nielsen, Karen Thøgersdatter, Maren Nielsdatter (carried by Søren Jacobsen's wife). 1787: age 10, with parents. 1801: age given as 14 [does not reconcile — see Open Items and `family-tree/possible-duplicates.md` B1]. 1834: age 57 (reconciles with b.1777), unmarried, living with half-brother Mads Pedersen Boe in Sdr. ## 04. Peder Christensen Boe (1816–1879) — Detailed Notes

**Vital facts:** born 18.5.1816, Jørsby, son of gårdbruger Christen Pedersen Boe (03) and Mette Jensdatter. Faddere: Mads Pedersen, Lars Toft (gårdbrugere, Jørsby/Sdr. Dråby), Christen og Zidsel Nygaard, Grethe Pedersdatter (Jørsby). Confirmed 1831, Jørsby, age 15, "Ganske gode Kundskaber. Opførsel også god." Moved as tjenestedreng: to Sdr. Dråby 3.11.1834 (age 18), to Sejerslev 2.11.1837 (age 21½), then to Fur 1848 ("Har tjent på Mølhave," "med godt skudsmål"). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.18-20]

**A discrepancy KEJ flags himself, not this session's finding:** the 1.2.1845 folketælling entry for "tjenestekarl Peder Christensen, 28 år" gives his birthplace as "Sdr Dråby" — the manuscript itself marks this with an exclamation point, since every other record in this section (and generation "03"'s own account of his birth) places his birth in Jørsby, not Sdr. Dråby. Transcribed faithfully as a source-level inconsistency KEJ already noticed, not queued to `grandfather-review/` since it isn't new information for him. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.18-20][CONTRADICTS: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, birth record earlier in same manuscript]

**Marriage:** 1.2.1850 folketælling shows Peder Christensen, 33, tjenestekarl for sognepræst Ernst Christian Fischer, Fur — where Bodil Kirstine Iversdatter also served as tjenestepige (in the parish since 1849). Married 17.8.1850, Fur: ungkarl Peder Christensen (tjener i præstegården) and pigen Bodil Kirstine Iversdatter, born 17.10.1824, Thorum i Salling, daughter of Iver Hendriksen and Else Jensdatter (manuscript cross-references "SØGÅRD-SLÆGTEN," an anetavle appendix not yet read — no individual records created for her parents here, same as Mette Jensdatter's parents in generation "03," pending that appendix). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.18-20]

**Property — a transcribed skøde (deed), a genuine primary-source example matching this manuscript's habit of quoting source documents at length:** 1.11.1851, Peder Christensen Boe bought a hedelod ("Anshede, bakkerne," matr. 130, hartkorn ¼ album) from selvejergårdmand Anders Andersen for 100 rigsbankdaler — full deed text transcribed in the manuscript, witnessed by Christen Mortensen and Søren Mortensen, tinglæst (registered) 5.8.1853 at Salling Herreders Ret. A later continuation-attest (1860) notes and resolves a title-chain irregularity in Anders Andersen's own prior ownership. The manuscript concludes Peder Boe likely took possession by 1.4.1852, shortly after the 1850 wedding — supported by son Iver's birth record (22.8.1851) already naming the father "Peder Christensen Boe af Heden." A second hedelod (No. 5, Litra B, Østerhusheden) was bought 30.6.1853 for 36 rigsbankdaler from husmand Jens Pedersen; both deeds registered together 5.8.1853. A panteobligation (mortgage bond) of 100 rigsdaler to ungkarl Mikkel Jensen (Sdr. Lundgård), secured against these hedelodder at 4% p.a., was issued 27.12.1863 and redeemed 14.12.1879 — the manuscript adds context that private mortgage financing of this kind was standard before parish savings banks (e.g. "Fuur Ø's Spare- og Laanekasse," established 1873) became common. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.18-20]

**Census and occupation:** 1.2.1855, daglejer i "Jegdal," wife and 2 children (Iver, 4; Christen, 2). 1.2.1860, husmand, "Heden," wife and 2 children. 1.2.1870, age 53, wife and daughter Mette (6). Boe (Lemvig), corroborated by a quoted *Skive Folkeblad* article (1.9.1947) — that Peder Boe worked as a gå-post (walking postman) between Skive and Fur, appointed by the parish priest, running the route twice weekly and also carrying wool/vadmel goods for dyeing and errands to the apothecary and shops. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.18-20]

**Death:** Peder Christensen Boe ("Hmd, gift, af Anshede") died 10.2.1879 on the Limfjord together with Christen Nielsen Hautorn (also married, "af Anshede") — per Mette Boe and K.K.K. Boe, they drove into a våge (open break in the ice) while sledging to Nykøbing Mors; Boe was recovered but froze to death. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.18-20]

## 05. Christen Pedersen Boe (1853–1930) — Detailed Notes

**Vital facts:** born 6.7.1853, "Heden," Fur sogn, son of Peder Christensen Boe (04) and Bodil Kirstine Iversdatter — same faddere as recorded in generation "04"'s own birth entry (Niels Thomsens kone of Hvirp, Ane Nielsdatter, Inger Nielsdatter, Jens Danielsen, Christen Mortensen, Gravers Nielsen). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.21-24]

**Marriage — KEJ flags the timing himself:** married 25.7.1872, Fur, age 19½ (the manuscript marks this with its own exclamation point, the same annotation habit already seen for the "Sdr Dråby" birthplace discrepancy in generation "04"), to 25-year-old Mette Jensen, born 27.2.1847, Debel, Fur, daughter of Jens Christian Thorsen and Margrethe Jensdatter (mother from an old Furbo family — manuscript cross-references "KAPITEL 9," not yet read). Forlovere: hmd. Jens Chr. Thorsen (Debel) and hmd. Peder Christensen Boe (Heden — Christen's own father). The eldest child was born 22.9.1872, only two months after the wedding, with the father recorded as "Indsidder" (lodger) in Debel at the time — consistent with the premarital-pregnancy account told below. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.21-24]

**Census and occupation trail, 1880–1930:** 1.2.1880, age 26, husfader og fisker in a house in "Blegager," with wife (32) and 3 children (a-c), plus his widowed mother (54). 1.2.1890, "Fisker" in "Bakken," wife and 8 children (a-h), plus mother Bodil Kirstine Iversdatter (66). Later moved to a small property in Nederby, eventually taken over by daughter Bodil Kirstine ("Stinne," child d.) — sold after her 1943 death to Peder Chr. Mikkelsen. 1.2.1901: fisker og agerbruger in Bakkerne, wife and 4 children at home (a, f, g, h), plus plejesøn ("foster/adopted grandson") Esper Marinus Christensen (=05-b-I., daughter b.'s son — his own fuller biography belongs to the Efterslægt section, not yet read); mother Bodil Kirstine listed as "Aftægtskone." 1.2.1906/1911: similar household, sons/daughters increasingly moved out, Esper Marinus still present as "Plejebarn." 1.2.1916: "Landmand," alone with wife (children grown and gone). 1.2.1921/1925/1930: Nederby, progressively "Landbruger" → "Alderdomsnyder" (old-age pensioner), with unmarried daughter Bodil/Kristine (d.) still at home as housekeeper. The husband was a husmand like his father, supplementing with Limfjord fishing; started with a heath plot in Nederby (one cow), later took over the family home and could keep a horse, cows, and sheep. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.21-24] — one aside: the 1.2.1930 entry names the household head as "Mette Pedersen Boe," not "Mette Jensen (Boe)" as she's named everywhere else in this section; reads as the husband's patronymic bleeding into hers in that one census transcription, not a genealogical fact — too trivial and contextually unambiguous to warrant a formal tracking entry.

**Death:** Christen Boe died 24.5.1930, Nederby; wife Mette Jensen died 24.12.1932, same place. "Chr. P. Boes Familiegravsted" (family grave) still stood at Fur churchyard as of 1980 per the manuscript. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.21-24]

**Family lore, quoted from grandson K.K.K. Boe (1976), characterized by KEJ himself as "en absolut pålidelig kilde (andenhånds)" — an absolutely reliable, secondhand source:** Mette worked as a trained seamstress at the parsonage where Chr. Boe served as thresher and coachman for the pastor (a predecessor of the Pastor Brandt mentioned earlier in the passage, per KEJ's own parenthetical clarification). During a carriage ride, the priest confronted Boe about visiting "his girl" at night and said he wouldn't have it — Boe didn't deny it, but by then it was already too late. A secondhand account has Boe seeking advice from "the old blacksmith" in Madsbad, who told him to present himself immediately to Mette's father, Jens Chr. Thordsen — a wealthy, respected carpenter and coachbuilder in Debel — and propose marriage, on condition Thordsen provide 4 tdr. land of heath and a fitted wagon as a bridal gift. Thordsen, impressed by the young man's boldness, agreed. Boe then became a soldier and they married during a furlough. Their first two children were born in the heath cottage; he later traded his way into the Bakkerne property, suffered a paralysis later in life, and moved to Nederby, where both died — the grandson-narrator attended both funerals. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.21-24] — a direct oral-history quotation, not a primary record; treat accordingly if this is ever promoted into a domain.

**Naming tradition and the 1904/05 royal name grant — a load-bearing legal/genealogical fact, not just color:** the eldest son (a., Jens Christian) was named after the *maternal* grandfather first — only the next son received the paternal grandfather's name (c., Jens Peder). More significantly: "Boe" had long been used only as an informal kaldenavn (both Peder Christensen Boe and Christen Pedersen were "Peder-"/"Christen Boe" in daily speech, not by legal surname) — securing the right to the name required a kongelig bevilling (royal permission), sought around 1904/05, apparently at son Laurids's urging (a murer/mason in Durup). Not everyone was included: the eldest son Jens Christian (a.) couldn't be included since contact with him in America had been lost; Jens Peder Christensen (c.) refused to be included, so that branch didn't get the name either (he later protested the "Chr. P. Boe" gravestone inscription); sister Else Marie (i.) had died by then. Six of the nine siblings received the name by royal bevilling in 1905 — sons Jens Boe and Laurids Boe, and daughter b.'s son Esper Marinus, are the ones noted as carrying the name forward. Manuscript cross-references "indledningen til Boe-slægten, kapitel 1 – afsnit 01" (the chapter's own introduction) for more, not yet read. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.21-24]

**Nine children of Christen Boe and Mette Jensen, 1872–1892 (a–i) — precise birthdates now confirmed against the Aneoversigt-level summary already in `family-tree/tree.ged`, no contradictions found there:**
- **a. Jens Christian Christensen**, b.22.9.1872, Fur (father "Indsidder i Debel" at the time). Emigrated to America ~1890; did not receive the Boe name in 1905 (contact lost).

- **d. Bodil (Boel) Kirstine Christensen Boe ("Stinne")**, b.22.7.1880, Fur (father "Husmand – af Heden"). Cared for her parents at home; inherited and later sold the Nederby property; died 1943.
