## Document Purpose

Working extraction notes from "2024 Boe-slægen fra Mors og Fur.docx" (KEJ's compiled Boe-slægt manuscript, Kapitel 1-4, version maj 2026), processed section by section per the human's explicit request — not the whole document at once. Project-layer working notes, not domain knowledge — no Boe domain exists yet (Phase 3 not started). Original manuscript now stored at `library/deep-wells/boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur-2024.docx`.

**Source citation** (per `authoring-guidelines.md` §9.5, adapted — this is KEJ's own unpublished manuscript, not a published article): Knud Erik Jakobsen (KEJ), Risum, "Boe-slægten, Mors/Fur, Kapitel 1-4," unpublished manuscript, maj 2026, accessed via Google Drive (file ID `1-3X1fR0bJ2qnMjkERx1LTPhn-jOCto29`), 2026-08-06. Registered as a deep well: `library/reference-index.md#boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur`. Citation slug used below: `boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur`.

**Citation and fidelity methodology (added v1.2):** every claim below now carries a `[VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.N]` or `p.N-M` tag, not just the one file-level citation this document opened with — a real gap when this eventually promotes into a domain, since re-attaching citations fact-by-fact after the fact is worse than doing it as each section is read. The page numbers used are the section-level ranges already known from the manuscript's own table of contents (e.g. generation "02" = p.5-8) — not finer, invented precision. This is deliberate: fabricating a specific page number for an individual claim would be worse than the coarser-but-honest range, since a false precise citation is harder to catch than an admittedly coarse one. Going forward, from generation "04" onward, capture the exact page break per claim as it's read (the source `.docx` shows page numbers directly) so later sections get true per-claim precision rather than a repeated range.

This also answers a separate question: what guards against Claude simply misreading or mis-paraphrasing KEJ's own text (distinct from `grandfather-review/`, which guards against Claude's own *inferences* being wrong, not plain misreads)? Two practices, already partly in use and now made explicit: (1) quote consequential atomic facts — dates, ages, named individuals, and any passage that reads as a direct kirkebog/skifte transcription — close to verbatim rather than paraphrasing them, so a mismatch is visible at a glance; (2) precise per-claim citations (this paragraph) make it a cheap, localized check for KEJ or the human to compare a specific claim against the original document, rather than needing a full independent re-transcription pass, which would cost roughly as much as the original extraction for uncertain benefit given it would be the same reader checking their own reading.

## 02. Peder Christensen Boe (o.1731–1810) — Detailed Notes

**Vital facts:** born o.1731 (place not stated); died 17.8.1810, Sdr. Dråby, age 79. Husmand (Jørsby, from 1775, tenant under Nygård estate) → fæstegårdmand (Sdr. Dråby, from ~1785, under Ullerup estate). Trolovet 29.12.1775; married (viet) 16.2.1776, Jørsby kirke, to Sidsel (Zidsel) Lauridsdatter Toft. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Fadder (godparent) appearances cited from Jørsby kirkebog:** 1771 (Mads Andersen "på Heden" & Margrethe Josephsdatter's son Anders, b.8.5); 1772 (Jens Sørensen Heede & Maren Larsdatter's daughter Maren, b.9.10 — together with future wife Zidsel); 1773 (Zidsel's own "illegitimate" son Mads, b.8.5 — see conflict below); 1774 and 1785 (Christen Larsen Toft & Inger Michelsdatter's son Michel, b.16.3). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Wife — Zidsel (Sidsel) Lauridsdatter Toft:** born o.1747, Jørsby, daughter of Laurids Nielsen Toft (d.1797). Confirmed 1765, age 18. Died 25.12.1821, Sdr. Dråby, age 66 (matches Aneoversigt). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**1787 folketælling** (Sdr. Dråby): Peder Christensen Boe, 60 [age doesn't reconcile cleanly with b. o.1731 → would be ~56; not resolved here], bonde/gårdbeboer; wife Zidsel Laursdatter, 38; 3 joint children (a–c, below); plus Zidsel's son Mads, 14, "hendes Søn avlet uden Ægteskab" (her son begotten outside marriage). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Notable documented history — the "Kyssesekten" (Kiss Sect):** A religious sect active in Jørsby led by Peder Lassen Grud, based on a literal reading of "greet one another with a holy kiss" (Paul's epistles). Peder Lassen fathered children by at least 4 women, was prosecuted, sentenced to labor at the Copenhagen fortress and estate forfeiture (later reduced on appeal), served ~2 years, then returned and continued the sect for some years. Zidsel Lauridsdatter Toft (later wife of Peder Christensen Boe, 02) was one of the women involved: her son Mads Pedersen was fathered by Peder Lassen out of wedlock — the manuscript quotes what reads like a direct kirkebog record naming Peder Lassen as declared father ("blef udlagt til Barne Fader"). Peder Boe himself fell away from the sect in 1773 and testified against Peder Lassen. Manuscript cites "Historisk Årbog for Thy og Mors" 1968-70 and Steen Busck's "En alternativ bevægelse i 1700-årene" as secondary sources for this episode. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Mads Pedersen (Boe)** — son of Zidsel Lauridsdatter Toft and Peder Lassen Grud (per the above), later took the name "Boe" after his stepfather Peder Christensen Boe. Married Karen Pedersdatter Krøll (banns 1799, Sdr. Dråby); took over stepfather's farm 1802; later remarried Karen Christensdatter (by 1834); died 10.11.1847, Sdr. Dråby, aftægtsmand, age 76. Descendants still carried the Boe name as of 2019 per the manuscript. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8] — birth year deliberately omitted here, see Open Items #1 and `grandfather-review/queue.md` #4.

**Boe surname origin — explicitly stated as unknown by KEJ himself:** the manuscript states plainly that the origin of the "Boe" name on Mors is not known, and explicitly considers and rejects (tentatively) a connection to a separate "Sallingboe" family in Sønder Dråby despite the name similarity, for lack of any documented common link. Worth noting this is KEJ's own methodological rigor already in evidence — he flags his own uncertainty rather than asserting a tidy origin story. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

**Children of 02 and Sidsel, b. 1777–1784, Jørsby (labeled a–c):**
- **a. Anna Pedersdatter Boe**, b. 29.3.1777, Jørsby. Faddere: Laurs Ammidsbøl, Michel Laursen, Ole Nielsen, Karen Thøgersdatter, Maren Nielsdatter (carried by Søren Jacobsen's wife). 1787: age 10, with parents. 1801: age given as 14 [does not reconcile — see Open Items and `family-tree/possible-duplicates.md` B1]. 1834: age 57 (reconciles with b.1777), unmarried, living with half-brother Mads Pedersen Boe in Sdr. Dråby. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]
- **b. Christen Pedersen Boe**, b. 21.6.1780, Jørsby. Faddere: Maren Nelsdatter Toft, Johanne Mourirsdatter, Christen Toft, Christen Thyboe, Johan Larsen (Nygård). Married 19.10.1808, Jørsby, to Mette Jensdatter (manuscript cross-references "kapitel 3 Afsnit 02 – h."). Gårdmand, Jørsby, from ~1808; died 11.3.1832, Jørsby. **This is generation "03" in the Aneoversigt** — consistent. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]
- **c. Laurs Pedersen Boe**, b. 9.7.1784, Jørsby. Faddere: Inger Michelsdatter, Anne Madsdatter, Niels Hald, Mickel Hald, Christen Thyboe. 1787: age 3 (reconciles). 1801: age given as 14 [does not reconcile — see Open Items and `family-tree/possible-duplicates.md` B2]. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.5-8]

## 03. Christen Pedersen Boe (1780–1832) — Detailed Notes

**Vital facts:** born 21.6.1780, Jørsby, son of Peder Christensen Boe (02) & Sidsel Laursdatter (husmand "Nyegaard tilhørende"). Married 1808, Jørsby (trolovet 8.9., viet 19.10.) to Mette Jensdatter, born Jørsby 27.4.1787, daughter of Jens Møller & Else Jensdatter (manuscript cross-references "KAPITEL 3"). Witnesses (forlovere): Niels Christensen and Mads Pedersen (his half-brother, per the previous section). Gårdfæster under Ullerup gods, Jørsby; died 11.3.1832, age 51. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.9-17]

**Probate record (skifte), transcribed at length in the manuscript** — a genuine primary-source example of the `skifteprotokol` citation type built in `authoring-guidelines.md` §9.5: proceedings dated 13 April, 15 December, and finalized 22 December 1832, Ullerup gods skifteprotokol / Herredscontoiret Nykøbing. Heirs: widow Mette Jensdatter and children Else (21, serving Hesselberg), Sidsel (19), Lars (18), Peder (16, serving with Jens Poulsen), Maren (12), Johanne (10), Grethe (3). Guardians: Mads Boe (sons), Lars Larsen (daughters); widow's lavværge: Hans Nielsen. Full itemized estate inventory given (livestock, furniture, farm equipment) — valuation 212 dr 4 mk 4 sk sølv. The estate was insolvent: total expenses 692 dr 2 mk 4 sk against that valuation, including a 210 rdr landlord claim for "Bygfæld, Besætning og Inventarium." Worth citing this specific passage precisely once a domain exists — it is exactly the kind of primary-source detail GPS rewards. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.9-17, Ullerup gods skifteprotokol 1832]

**Widow Mette Jensdatter:** 1834 folketælling, 47, widow/tenant-farmer's-wife under Ullerup, with 5 children at home. 1845 and 1850: aftægtskone (retirement boarder) with son Lars in Jørsby. Died 19.10.1850, Jørsby, age 63½, "Slagtilfælde" (stroke). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.9-17]

## Open Items — Data Quality

Not resolved here; flagged rather than silently corrected, per this repo's correction discipline. Items 3, 5, and 6 below (the likely-extraction-artifact anomalies) are now also tracked centrally in `family-tree/possible-duplicates.md` §2, cross-referenced by ID (A1-A3, B1-B3) — that file is where to look for the running count across sections, not just this one.

1. **Mads Pedersen (Boe)'s birth year is internally inconsistent within this single manuscript** — stated as 1774 in one passage ("hun i 1774 fødte sønnen Mads Pedersen (1774-1847)"), as 8.5.1773 in another ("Mads Pedersen, der er født 8.5.1773"), and implied as 1772, 1773, or 1771 by three different age-at-date statements (1801 age 29, 1834 age 61, 1847 death age 76). This reads as a genuine period-record ambiguity (common when a person's own documented age varied across census/record years), not a transcription error on this repo's part — **added to `grandfather-review/queue.md` as item 4**, since KEJ is best placed to know which year his own research ultimately settled on, if any.
2. **Possible manuscript mislabel:** a later passage refers to "Peder Christensen Boe (= 03)," restating facts identical to "02" (same birth year o.1731, same death date 17.8.1810). This looks like a typo (should likely read "= 02") rather than a second person, but not assumed — noted here, not queued separately, as a minor editorial question to mention alongside item 4 rather than a distinct finding.
3. **Anna Pedersdatter Boe's and Laurs Pedersen Boe's 1801 ages (both recorded as "14") don't reconcile with their birth years** (Anna would be ~24, Laurs ~17). Deliberately *not* flagged as a research inconsistency — this pattern (both entries showing the identical, wrong-for-both value) reads more like a text-extraction artifact from the original table layout than a real error in KEJ's source. Needs checking against the actual document formatting once viewed directly, not assumed to be a genuine period-record problem.
4. **Mette Jensdatter's birth year shows as "1878" in the Aneoversigt table**, which cannot be correct for someone married in 1808 — almost certainly a typo for 1778 or similar. Not corrected here; worth a quick confirmation. (Her actual birth date is later given precisely as 27.4.1787 in generation "03"'s own section — the Aneoversigt table's "1878"/"1778" is very likely just a stray digit-transposition of 1787, not a separate error.)
5. **Maren Christensdatter's (e.) death year shows as "1919"** and **Johanne Christensdatter's (g.) birth year shows as "1921"** — both born ~1818-1821 and clearly died/were born as infants in that era, not the 20th century. Reads as a recurring "19" vs "18" digit-transposition pattern in this extraction (third instance of this general shape of error, after items above) — worth double-checking a few more instances before concluding it's systematic, but treating as a likely extraction/OCR artifact rather than a manuscript error unless the pattern keeps recurring.
6. **Johanne Christensdatter (h.)'s age at the 1845 census (16) doesn't reconcile with her 1822 birth year** (would be ~22-23). Same low-confidence, not-yet-queued treatment as the earlier 1801 age discrepancies.
