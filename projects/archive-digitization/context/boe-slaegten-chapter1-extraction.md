## 02. Peder Christensen Boe (o.1731–1810) — Detailed Notes

**Vital facts:** born o.1731 (place not stated); died 17.8.1810, Sdr. Dråby, age 79. Husmand (Jørsby, from 1775, tenant under Nygård estate) → fæstegårdmand (Sdr. Dråby, from ~1785, under Ullerup estate). Trolovet 29.12.1775; married (viet) 16.2.1776, Jørsby kirke, to Sidsel (Zidsel) Lauridsdatter Toft.

**Fadder (godparent) appearances cited from Jørsby kirkebog:** 1771 (Mads Andersen "på Heden" & Margrethe Josephsdatter's son Anders, b.8.5); 1772 (Jens Sørensen Heede & Maren Larsdatter's daughter Maren, b.9.10 — together with future wife Zidsel); 1773 (Zidsel's own "illegitimate" son Mads, b.8.5 — see conflict below); 1774 and 1785 (Christen Larsen Toft & Inger Michelsdatter's son Michel, b.16.3).

**Wife — Zidsel (Sidsel) Lauridsdatter Toft:** born o.1747, Jørsby, daughter of Laurids Nielsen Toft (d.1797). Confirmed 1765, age 18. Died 25.12.1821, Sdr. Dråby, age 66 (matches Aneoversigt).

**1787 folketælling** (Sdr. Dråby): Peder Christensen Boe, 60 [age doesn't reconcile cleanly with b. o.1731 → would be ~56; not resolved here], bonde/gårdbeboer; wife Zidsel Laursdatter, 38; 3 joint children (a–c, below); plus Zidsel's son Mads, 14, "hendes Søn avlet uden Ægteskab" (her son begotten outside marriage).

**Notable documented history — the "Kyssesekten" (Kiss Sect):** A religious sect active in Jørsby led by Peder Lassen Grud, based on a literal reading of "greet one another with a holy kiss" (Paul's epistles). Peder Lassen fathered children by at least 4 women, was prosecuted, sentenced to labor at the Copenhagen fortress and estate forfeiture (later reduced on appeal), served ~2 years, then returned and continued the sect for some years. Zidsel Lauridsdatter Toft (later wife of Peder Christensen Boe, 02) was one of the women involved: her son Mads Pedersen was fathered by Peder Lassen out of wedlock — the manuscript quotes what reads like a direct kirkebog record naming Peder Lassen as declared father ("blef udlagt til Barne Fader"). Peder Boe himself fell away from the sect in 1773 and testified against Peder Lassen. Manuscript cites "Historisk Årbog for Thy og Mors" 1968-70 and Steen Busck's "En alternativ bevægelse i 1700-årene" as secondary sources for this episode.

**Mads Pedersen (Boe)** — son of Zidsel Lauridsdatter Toft and Peder Lassen Grud (per the above), later took the name "Boe" after his stepfather Peder Christensen Boe. Married Karen Pedersdatter Krøll (banns 1799, Sdr. Dråby); took over stepfather's farm 1802; later remarried Karen Christensdatter (by 1834); died 10.11.1847, Sdr. Dråby, aftægtsmand, age 76. Descendants still carried the Boe name as of 2019 per the manuscript.

**Boe surname origin — explicitly stated as unknown by KEJ himself:** the manuscript states plainly that the origin of the "Boe" name on Mors is not known, and explicitly considers and rejects (tentatively) a connection to a separate "Sallingboe" family in Sønder Dråby despite the name similarity, for lack of any documented common link. Worth noting this is KEJ's own methodological rigor already in evidence — he flags his own uncertainty rather than asserting a tidy origin story.

**Children of 02 and Sidsel, b. 1777–1784, Jørsby (labeled a–c):**
- **a. Anna Pedersdatter Boe**, b. 29.3.1777, Jørsby. Faddere: Laurs Ammidsbøl, Michel Laursen, Ole Nielsen, Karen Thøgersdatter, Maren Nielsdatter (carried by Søren Jacobsen's wife). 1787: age 10, with parents. 1801: age given as 14 [does not reconcile — see Open Items]. 1834: age 57 (reconciles with b.1777), unmarried, living with half-brother Mads Pedersen Boe in Sdr. Dråby.
- **b. Christen Pedersen Boe**, b. 21.6.1780, Jørsby. Faddere: Maren Nelsdatter Toft, Johanne Mourirsdatter, Christen Toft, Christen Thyboe, Johan Larsen (Nygård). Married 19.10.1808, Jørsby, to Mette Jensdatter (manuscript cross-references "kapitel 3 Afsnit 02 – h."). Gårdmand, Jørsby, from ~1808; died 11.3.1832, Jørsby. **This is generation "03" in the Aneoversigt** — consistent.
- **c. Laurs Pedersen Boe**, b. 9.7.1784, Jørsby. Faddere: Inger Michelsdatter, Anne Madsdatter, Niels Hald, Mickel Hald, Christen Thyboe. 1787: age 3 (reconciles). 1801: age given as 14 [does not reconcile — see Open Items].

## Open Items — Data Quality

Not resolved here; flagged rather than silently corrected, per this repo's correction discipline.

1. **Mads Pedersen (Boe)'s birth year is internally inconsistent within this single manuscript** — stated as 1774 in one passage ("hun i 1774 fødte sønnen Mads Pedersen (1774-1847)"), as 8.5.1773 in another ("Mads Pedersen, der er født 8.5.1773"), and implied as 1772, 1773, or 1771 by three different age-at-date statements (1801 age 29, 1834 age 61, 1847 death age 76). This reads as a genuine period-record ambiguity (common when a person's own documented age varied across census/record years), not a transcription error on this repo's part — **added to `grandfather-review/queue.md` as item 4**, since KEJ is best placed to know which year his own research ultimately settled on, if any.
2. **Possible manuscript mislabel:** a later passage refers to "Peder Christensen Boe (= 03)," restating facts identical to "02" (same birth year o.1731, same death date 17.8.1810). This looks like a typo (should likely read "= 02") rather than a second person, but not assumed — noted here, not queued separately, as a minor editorial question to mention alongside item 4 rather than a distinct finding.
3. **Anna Pedersdatter Boe's and Laurs Pedersen Boe's 1801 ages (both recorded as "14") don't reconcile with their birth years** (Anna would be ~24, Laurs ~17). Deliberately *not* flagged as a research inconsistency — this pattern (both entries showing the identical, wrong-for-both value) reads more like a text-extraction artifact from the original table layout than a real error in KEJ's source. Needs checking against the actual document formatting once viewed directly, not assumed to be a genuine period-record problem.
4. **Mette Jensdatter's birth year shows as "1878" in the Aneoversigt table**, which cannot be correct for someone married in 1808 — almost certainly a typo for 1778 or similar. Not corrected here; worth a quick confirmation.
