## Chapter 02 — Daniel Knudsen Hopp (o.1681-1730/31), "01-a"

The eldest son of the root figure, Knud Danielsen Hopp — and a genuinely interesting case of upward mobility followed by a hard fall. Started as fuldmægtig (agent) on the Astrup estate from 1711, ridefoged (estate steward) from 1714, under first kaptajn Mogens Friis and then major (later oberstløjtnant) Christian Kruse. In 1716 he personally bought a farm in Vium, Hjerk sogn for 210 rigsdaler and sold it the following year for 300 — both deeds are quoted in full, original wording, in the source. His position collapsed along with his employer's finances: Christian Kruse's own affairs were in serious disorder (he'd pledged the manor house itself and 130 oxen as first-priority collateral on a loan, highly unusual for the time), and after a lawsuit and a 1725 court judgment, Kruse lost Astrup to forced auction. Daniel Knudsen Hopp is subsequently found only as "Daniel Hop i Gamstrup," a tenant under a different, smaller estate (Nørgaard), still occasionally called on as a vitterlighedsvidne (witness of record) on local deeds through 1728. He died poor, in a small house in Vester Grønning, sometime between his last known signature (31 May 1730) and 24 February 1731, when a lease document already refers to him as "Salig" (the late).

His wife, **Bodil Nielsdatter** (`#I367`), has her own striking backstory: before her marriage she was a serving girl for Anna Cathrine Akeleye, an impoverished noblewoman at Stubbergård (a former nunnery in Sevel sogn) who had to abandon the estate after bad harvests in 1704-05 and died poor in Norway around 1707. Bodil married Daniel Knudsen Hopp the following year.

**3 children, all modeled:**
- **a. Niels Danielsen Hopp** (`#I369`) — the direct-line continuation, see chapter 03 below.
- **b. Anne Berthe Danielsdatter Hopp** (`#I716`) — lived with her brother Niels in the Vedersø degnebolig, died there unmarried in 1759, 48 years old.
- **c. Knud Danielsen Hopp** (`#I717`) — a genuinely different life path: became a værtshusholder (innkeeper) in Viborg in 1765 and, per the source's own dry aside, "meget passende" ("very fitting"), also a police officer. Married Magdalene Hjardemaal (`#I718`) in 1744 — likely a sister of Nicolai Hjardemaal, the Thorum degn who married a Hopp cousin from the 01C line (Dorthe Nielsdatter Hopp), a second cross-connection between the two families. Died childless in 1777; his unusually detailed, fully-transcribed probate inventory is the **only surviving source** for how many children his brother Niels (the direct-line degn in Vedersø) actually had — Vedersø's own parish register is burned, so this secondhand mention ("een søn 2de Døttre") is genuinely load-bearing for chapter 03's own family reconstruction.

`family-tree/tree.ged`: `#I366`, `#I367` enriched; `#F122` enriched (marriage date refined to 17 Jun 1708, 2 new children added); `#I716`, `#I717`, `#I718` new; `#F237` new.

## Chapter 03 — Niels Danielsen Hopp (1707/1709-1775), "02-a"

The direct-line continuation. Served as a young man on the same Astrup estate his father had worked, signing as vitterlighedsvidne on several lease documents 1728-1730. From 1732 he was in service to Christen Linde at the manor Åberg in Ringkøbing amt — and from that same year administered most of the estate's probate settlements and kept the probate protocol himself until 1761, alongside his actual job. Called to be degn (parish clerk) in Vedersø in 1733, a post he held for 42 years under four different priests, until his death in 1775 — succeeded directly by his own son, Daniel (chapter 04). Notably, of the roughly twelve Hopp-family degne/skoleholdere active in the 1700s, only one was ever "studiosus" (university-trained) — and it wasn't Niels or his son.

**A genuine, unresolved internal inconsistency worth flagging plainly:** the "01"-overview table and this chapter's own title both give his birth year as **1707**, but the chapter's own body text cites a specific baptismal record: "døbt 6.10.1709 i Rybjerg." No attempt was made here to decide which is right — both are from KEJ's own material, just in tension with each other.

Married **Christence Thøgersdatter** (`#I370`) in 1732 (trolovet 24 October, married 16 November, in Estvad) — daughter of Thøger Thomsen "Kusk" and Johanne Pedersdatter, who had herself been in service at Estvadgård under a secretary named Benzon from 1727. Her own baptismal date, as given in the source (29 February 1702), is calendrically impossible — 1702 wasn't a leap year — but transcribed as stated rather than corrected.

**3 children** — known only secondhand, since Vedersø's own parish register is burned prior to 1813, via his brother Knud's 1777 Viborg probate ("een søn 2de Døttre"):
- **a. Daniel Nielsen Hopp** (`#I371`) — the direct-line continuation to chapter 04 (his own dedicated 59-page chapter, received and stored but not yet read — Phase 3b). Born 30 November 1732 in Estvad, home-baptized by his own grandmother.
- **b. Karen Nielsdatter Hopp** (`#I719`) — no birth or death data survives; known only from an incidental detail in her brother Daniel's first wife's 1793 probate, which mentions a chest previously gifted by "sin faster, Karen Hopp, som ved døden var afgået" (her aunt Karen Hopp, by then deceased). Presumably unmarried, no husband mentioned anywhere.
- **c. N.N. Nielsdatter Hopp** (`#I720`) — completely unknown, not even a first name. KEJ's own text speculates she might have been named for one of the grandmothers (Bodil or Johanne) and was probably older than Karen — explicitly labeled as a guess, not a finding.

`family-tree/tree.ged`: `#I369`, `#I370`, `#I371` enriched; `#F123` enriched (marriage date refined to 16 Nov 1732, 2 new children added); `#I719`, `#I720` new.
