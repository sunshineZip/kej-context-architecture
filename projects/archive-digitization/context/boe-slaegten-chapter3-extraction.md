## Document Purpose

Working extraction notes for Kapitel 3 ("Boe-slægten, Vester Vandet") of "2024 Boe-slægen fra Mors og Fur.docx" — one file per chapter, per the convention started with `boe-slaegten-chapter2-extraction.md`. Same source, same citation slug, same fidelity discipline.

**Source citation:** Knud Erik Jakobsen (KEJ), Risum, "Boe-slægten, Mors/Fur, Kapitel 1-4," unpublished manuscript, maj 2026. Registered as a deep well: `library/reference-index.md#boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur`.

**What Chapter 3 is:** like Chapter 2, this isn't a new descendant branch — it's the ancestry behind another spouse Chapter 1 named without detail. Mette Jensdatter (`@I10@`, generation "03"'s wife, married to Christen Pedersen Boe `@I4@`) is the daughter of Jens Christensen Møller, a miller's son from Vester Vandet parish whose own parents both died within weeks of each other in 1743, orphaning him as an infant. Chapter 3 traces the Møller (miller) family back two more generations from there. Spans p.66-73.

**Source character, again worth flagging:** this chapter is, if anything, more uncertain than Chapter 2 — the source's own section headings for the two oldest generations are literally marked with a question mark ("Christen Christensen Møller ?"), reflecting KEJ's own doubt about whether records for a "Christen Christensen Møller" in Øster Mølle and a same-named "Christen Christensen Møller" in Klovborg (one generation apart) are being correctly distinguished from each other and from at least one other contemporary of the same name. Ages given for the same recorded death sometimes disagree within the same passage (see `@I308@`'s note). This extraction transcribes what the source states, flags what it marks uncertain, and doesn't force a cleaner picture than the source itself has.

**A significant cross-chapter find in this checkpoint:** while checking for Chapter-3-relevant images, one turned out to be KEJ's own handwritten master pedigree chart spanning all four chapters (`image37.png`) — not a narrative source, but his own working genealogical summary. It directly bears on a contradiction found in this chapter's own text (see Open Items and `grandfather-review/queue.md` item 9) and will likely be useful again once Chapter 4 and the Anetavler (which the chart also covers) are read.

**No `[SENSITIVE]` tagging needed** — everyone in this chapter died in the 18th or early 19th century.

## 01. Christen Christensen Møller (?), o.1711 — Detailed Notes

Møller (miller) at Øster Mølle, Vester Vandet sogn, from o.1711. Trolovet 28.6.1703, viet 7.10.1703, Vester Vandet kirke, to Karen Madsdatter ("ved Møllen"). Nine children known, "a."–"i." — the source itself notes birth records are missing for 1710-1719, which is why several of these children have no birth date at all, only a fadder-list appearance. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.67-68]

**Nine children:**
- **a. Christen Christensen** (b.4.8.1704, Klitmøller, "Klokken 8 Formiddag").
- **b. Anders Christensen** (b.7.1.1707, Klitmøller).
- **c. Maren Christensdatter** (b.10.6.1709, Klitmøller).
- **d. Niels Christensen** (b.1711; d.1.8.1742, age 31, "Christen Christensen Møllers søn i Øster Mølle"). Buried 7.8.1742 alongside his sister Anne (f.).
- **e. Christen Christensen Møller** (b. o.1713; d.15.8.1743) — see "02" below; this is Jens Christensen Møller's own father.
- **f. Anne Christensdatter Møller** (b.1714; d.30.7.1742, age 28y5m4d, "Christen Christensen Møllers datter i Øster Mølle"). Buried the same day as her brother Niels (d.).
- **g. Karen Christensdatter Møller** — no birth/death date in the source (likely among the 1710-1719 gap years); known only as a fadder for her brother Christen's (e.) family in 1738.
- **h. Ingeborg Christensdatter Møller** (b.1717; d.25.5.1740, age 23y23w). Also fadder for brother Christen's family, 1741.
- **i. Anders Christensen Møller** — no birth/death date; known only as a fadder for brother Christen's family, 1741, a second "Anders" alongside "b." above.

[VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.67-68]

## 02. Christen Christensen Møller (?), o.1713–1743 — Detailed Notes

Son "e." of the above. Trolovet 20.9.1737, viet 16.10.1737, Vester Vandet kirke, to Karen Pedersdatter Schindrup, "Klovborg ved Klitmøller." **An internal age contradiction, not resolved:** his death (15.8.1743) is recorded once as "i sin Alders 39 Aar og 1 Dag" and once as "i sin Alder 29 år og 1 Dag" — 29 fits his o.1713 birth better, but the source states both. His wife Karen died just three weeks earlier, 27.7.1743, age 30. With both parents dead within weeks of each other, their infant son Jens (below) was "formodentlig kommet i pleje hos familie" (presumably fostered by relatives) and doesn't appear in Vester Vandet's own records again. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.69-70]

**Four children, "a."–"d.":**
- **a. Anne Christensdatter** (b.19.6.1738, Klovborg, "1 Time før Solens opgang").
- **b. Karen Christensdatter** (b.19.9.1739, Klovborg).
- **c. Peder Christensen Møller** (b.29.6.1741, Klovborg; d.10.1.1794, Klostermølle). Moved to Nykøbing Mors, "Markmand" (uncertain reading, marked "?" in the source). Married Caritas (Elisabeth) Sejersdatter (b. o.1748; d.3.3.1790, Nykøbing, age 42). Acted as forlover (witness) for his brother Jens's 1773 betrothal in Elsø. Small age discrepancies recur for Peder himself (the 1787 census gives 47, implying b. o.1740; his own death record gives 49, implying b. o.1745) — neither corrected here, both noted as the same low-confidence imprecision already common in this chapter. Five children, "I."–"V.": Birgitte Pedersdatter (1768), Sejer Pedersen (1771), Maren/Marie Pedersdatter (1774; d.1784, age 10), and two daughters both named Ide Pedersdatter — the first (b. Dec 1777) died 1778 at 30 weeks, the second (b.12.4.1781) died 1782 at 12 weeks, a name reused after an infant death, the same pattern seen throughout this manuscript.
- **d. Jens Christensen Møller** — see "03" below; Mette Jensdatter's (`@I10@`) own father.

**A cluster of likely digit-transcription errors, all in Peder's own children's records:** the source's plain text gives Maren/Marie's birth year as "1874," and the second Ide's birth/death years as "1881"/"2782" — all chronologically impossible given each child's own stated age at death, and all corrected in `tree.ged` to the clearly-intended reading (1774, 1781, 1782) without a queue entry, the same treatment as other unambiguous digit slips elsewhere in this manuscript. Three such errors clustered in one small family, in one part of the document, reads as a localized artifact of this session's own text extraction from that specific page, not a manuscript error. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.69-70]

## 03. Jens Christensen Møller (o.1743/44–1796) — Detailed Notes

Child "d." of the above, orphaned in infancy. Confirmed 1761, Sejerslev kirke, "født i Vester Vandet sogn," then moved to Nørre Dråby. Fæstegårdmand (tenant farmer) in Jørsby, under Ullerup gods, from his first marriage onward. **This is Mette Jensdatter's (`@I10@`) own father** — Chapter 1's `@F15@` placeholder (created during generation "03"'s reading, with no individual records, "to avoid guessing name forms not seen directly") is now refined into a full family record. Died 27.1.1796, age 54 (implying b. o.1742, a minor discrepancy from the o.1743/44 given elsewhere — not resolved). [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.71-73]

**First marriage, to Anne Jensdatter Overgaard** (trolovet 21.6.1773, viet 12.10.1773, Elsø kirke; d. — the source's own text reads "1876," chronologically impossible against a 1773 marriage, read as 1776, matching Jens's documented remarriage that same year). Two sons, both named **Jens Jensen Møller** — one born 19.8.1774, died 28.9.1774 at 5 weeks; the second born 13.1.1776, died at 1 week 2 days (the source's own death-date text, "1774," predates the 1776 birth and is read here as 1776).

**Second marriage, to Else Jensdatter** (b. o.1744/45, Vadstrup; d.22.8.1809, Jørsby) — trolovet 28.7.1776, viet 1.10.1776, Tødsø kirke. Five children:
- **c. Ole Christian Jensen Møller** (b.4.5.1777). Confirmed 1794, "hands Oplysnings Carakt.er ringe" (poor educational character, the priest's own phrase).
- **[Mette Jensdatter Møller, first of the name]** (b.17.4.1784; d.15.8.1784, infant, 18 days). The name was reused three years later for the sister who would go on to marry Christen Pedersen Boe (`@I10@`) — a sad but very typical instance of the period pattern already seen throughout this manuscript.
- **e. Anna Jensdatter Møller** (b.24.7.1779; d.24.7.1809, on her own 30th birthday). The 1801 census records her as "Aldeles vanvittig" (completely insane, the period's own clinical term); her death record reads "Som var berøvet Forstandens Brug" (who had been deprived of the use of reason). Transcribed factually, as a documented historical fact about a person two centuries deceased — not a `[SENSITIVE]` matter in the privacy sense that term is used elsewhere in this repo.
- **f. Maren Jensdatter Møller** (b.3.1.1782; d.16.8.1809, age 28). Confirmed 1800, "hos sin stedfar Jens Møller" — her stepfather, Else's second husband Jens Pedersen (see below), not her own late father.
- **Mette Jensdatter** (b.27.4.1787) — this is `@I10@`, already fully modeled from Chapter 1's generation "03." This chapter adds her parents in full, a full sibling group, and one significant new fact: **before marrying Christen Pedersen Boe, Mette had a son out of wedlock**, Jens Thomasen — see Open Items below for the date contradiction attached to this record.

**A letter-numbering inconsistency in the source itself, not resolved:** the marriage summary states "Børn i 2. ægteskab: c. – g." (five letters), but the 1787 census entry for the surviving children explicitly uses the letter "h." for one of them — a letter that falls outside the stated c.-g. range. Not force-resolved here; each child above is labeled with the source's own explicit letter where one is given.

**Else Jensdatter remarried** 5.6.1796 (the same year Jens died), to **Jens Pedersen**, a gårdbeboer in Jørsby — the betrothal announcement is quoted at length in the source, a genuine primary-document flavor (a formal attestation that no kinship, prior betrothal, or other impediment barred the marriage). KEJ himself speculates, without confirming, that a "Jens Møller" gårdmand buried 3.8.1809 in Jørsby (age 49y2w) might actually be this same Jens Pedersen under a surname mix-up — noted as his own open question, not this session's finding.

[VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.71-73]

## Open Items — Data Quality

1. **Christen Christensen Møller's (`@I308@`) age at death is given twice in the source, inconsistently:** "39 Aar og 1 Dag" in one place, "29 år og 1 Dag" in another, for the same 15.8.1743 death. 29 fits his own o.1713 birth better; not corrected in `tree.ged`, both readings noted in his record.
2. **A cluster of three digit-transcription errors** in Peder Christensen Møller's children's birth/death years (1874→1774, 1881→1781, 2782→1782) — read as unambiguous slips and corrected without a queue entry, consistent with this manuscript's other clear digit-transposition cases (`family-tree/possible-duplicates.md` sub-pattern A).
3. **A letter-numbering inconsistency for Jens Christensen Møller's five children with Else Jensdatter:** the source states the range as "c.–g." but separately uses the letter "h." for a surviving child in the 1787 census — the two don't reconcile. Not resolved; each child is labeled with whatever explicit letter the source gives it, nothing forced.
4. **The most significant item — escalated to `grandfather-review/queue.md` as item 9:** Christen Pedersen Boe and Mette Jensdatter's own wedding year is internally inconsistent across the manuscript — Chapter 1's prose gives 1808, Chapter 3's gives 1810 (same day/month, 19 October). A third source, KEJ's own handwritten master pedigree chart (`image37.png`), also shows 1810 — `tree.ged`'s `@F5@` record has been updated to use 1810 as the better-supported value, but this is not treated as fully settled without KEJ's own confirmation.
5. **A related, unresolved date contradiction:** Mette's out-of-wedlock son Jens Thomasen has a baptism date (9.7.1810) that falls *after* his own stated burial date (14.7.1809) — impossible as given. Not resolved; flagged alongside item 4 above in the same `grandfather-review/queue.md` entry, since both concern dates in the same narrow window of Mette's life just before her marriage.

Items 1-3 are not queued to `grandfather-review/queue.md` for the same reason given in the Chapter 2 notes: they're internal ambiguities in 18th-century parish-register fragments, predating anything KEJ researched himself, not decisions he's positioned to resolve. Items 4-5 are different — they involve a marriage KEJ documented in detail from multiple angles across his own manuscript, so he's well placed to know (or check) which date is correct.
