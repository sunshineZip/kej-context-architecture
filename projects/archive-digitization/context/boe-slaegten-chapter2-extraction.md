## Document Purpose

Working extraction notes for Kapitel 2 ("Boe-slægten, Jørsby") of "2024 Boe-slægen fra Mors og Fur.docx" — a separate file from `boe-slaegten-chapter1-extraction.md` now that Chapter 1 is complete, per `knowledge/domains/authoring-guidelines.md` §9.2 (one working-notes file per manuscript chapter, not one ever-growing file). Same source, same citation slug, same fidelity discipline (per-claim `[VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.N]` tags, verbatim quotes for atomic facts, no invented precision). Project-layer working notes, not domain knowledge.

**Source citation:** Knud Erik Jakobsen (KEJ), Risum, "Boe-slægten, Mors/Fur, Kapitel 1-4," unpublished manuscript, maj 2026. Registered as a deep well: `library/reference-index.md#boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur`.

**What Chapter 2 is:** not a new branch of the family, but the ancestry behind two people Chapter 1 already named without detail. Sidsel (Zidsel) Lauridsdatter Toft (`@I2@`, generation "02"'s wife) is herself the daughter of Laurids Nielsen Toft — Chapter 2 gives his own parents, siblings, and her three siblings' full descendant lines. Chapter 2 spans p.55-65 of the manuscript.

**A difference in source character worth flagging up front:** unlike Chapter 1's Efterslægt (clean, KEJ's own direct compilation, modern and well-organized), Chapter 2 is dense 18th-century kirkebog (parish register) fragments — short entries, frequent name reuse across siblings without always recording the earlier bearer's death, and occasional internal date conflicts. KEJ flags this himself in the chapter's own Aneoversigt introduction: **"Toft-familiens familierelationer kan være noget komplicerede! Efterslægten kan for nogle børn være noget usikker!"** ("The Toft family's family relationships can be somewhat complicated! The descendancy may be somewhat uncertain for some children!") — quoted here because it directly bears on how much confidence to place in this chapter's roman-numeral/child-ordering claims. Where the source doesn't state a child's position explicitly, this extraction infers it from birth order and says so in the individual's own `tree.ged` note, rather than presenting an inferred position as if the source stated it.

**A structural note, not an anomaly:** every individual in this chapter died well over a century ago — no `[SENSITIVE]` tagging is needed anywhere in Chapter 2.

## 01. Niels Toft (o.1706/1713–1797) — Detailed Notes

**Vital facts, with a source-acknowledged discrepancy:** at the 1.2.1787 folketælling, Niels Toft is 81 (→ b. o.1706), husfolk/betler ("poor folk, and beggars"), with wife Johanne Pedersdatter, also 81 (→ b. o.1706), and daughter Maren Nielsdatter (33, gift/skrædder — a different Maren, not one of Niels's own children named below). Niels drowned "natten mellem 4. og 5. November 1797... i en lille Aa imellem Torup og Knudby" ("in a small stream between Torup and Knudby"), was carried home by his sons, buried in Jørsby 12.11.1797, age 84 (→ b. o.1713) — a 7-year gap between the two source-stated birth years, presented by the manuscript itself as uncertain, not resolved here. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.56-58]

**Five children, "a."–"e.":**
- **a. Kirsten Nielsdatter Toft** (b. o.1749). Confirmed 1766, Sejerslev kirke, then service in Sejerslev. No further detail.
- **b. Maren Nielsdatter Toft** (b. o.1753; d. 24.4.1812, "almisselem," a poor-relief recipient). Confirmed 1770, Jørsby kirke. The source itself, tentatively, wonders whether she's the same Maren Nielsdatter Toft who was a fadder for Christen Pedersen Boe (`@I4@`) and Mette Jensdatter in Jørsby in 1780 — not confirmed.
- **c. Laurids Nielsen Toft** — see "02" below; Sidsel's own father.
- **d. Jens Nielsen Toft** — see his own subsection below; the largest sub-branch of this section.
- **e. Anders Nielsen Toft**. Confirmed 1778, Jørsby kirke. No further detail in the section read.

[VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.56-58]

**Jens Nielsen Toft** (b. o.1754, Jørsby; d. 9.12.1804, Jørsby) married Maren Svenningsdatter ("Snedker"). Nine children, "I."–"IX.," born 1781-1803:
- **I. Peder Jensen Toft** (b.9.10.1781).
- **II. Maren Jensdatter Toft** (b. o.1782).
- A second son also named **Peder Jensen Toft** (b.5.4.1785) — no death date is given in the section read for the first Peder, so it's unclear whether this is a name-reuse after an unrecorded infant death or two brothers alive at once with the same name. Confirmed 1803, Sejerslev kirke, "Mådelig oplyst" ("moderately educated").
- **IV. Johanna Jensdatter Toft** (b.25.10.1787; d.15.1.1827, "hjemme hos sin moder og søster Maren Toft").
- **V. Anna Jensdatter Toft** (b.19.3.1790; d.11.5.1794, infant, age 4y1m).
- **VI. Sissel (Zidsel) Jensdatter Toft** (b.14.8.1792). Married twice: Peder Nielsen (1st, 17.11.1818) and, after his death, Søren Christensen (2nd, 4.4.1836, Jørsby, she 42/he 29).
- **VII. Svenning Jensen Toft** (b.16.7.1795).
- **VIII. Maren Jensdatter Toft** (b.10.11.1798, døbt). Married Niels Christensen Toft, murermester (master mason), 8.4.1822, Jørsby.
- **IX. Anna Jensdatter Toft** (b.26.7.1801, døbt). Married Peder Christian Jacobsen, from Hørdum i Thy, 8.4.1822 (same day as her sister VIII's wedding). A younger, unnamed daughter was born and died the same day, 24.7.1803 — no individual record created.

## 02. Laurids Nielsen Toft (o.1754–) — Detailed Notes

Married Kirsten Jensdatter (d.6.2.1780, Jørsby, "gårdmand i Jørsby af Ullerup gods, hans gamle moder," age 77). Fæstegårdmand, Jørsby. Laurids himself appears as a fadder for Christen Pedersen Boe (`@I4@`, generation "03") in 1811, 1813, and 1814 — a direct, dated confirmation that the Toft and Boe families stayed in contact a full generation after Sidsel's own 1776 marriage. [VERIFIED: boe-slaegten-fra-mors-og-fur, p.59-65]

**Four children, "a."–"d.":**

**a. Christen Lauritsen Toft** (b. o.1744; d.19.3.1799, buried as "skoleholder og forsanger," schoolteacher and precentor) married Inger Michelsdatter (trolovet 17.1., viet 14.2.1771, Jørsby kirke; d.7.2.1802, "skoleholder enke," age 57). The source itself weighs whether the gårdmand ("farmer") and the later skoleholder ("schoolteacher") mentioned in different records are the same person — treated as probable, not certain. Twelve children, "I."–"XII.," 1771–o.1788 (only I, II, III, IV, and VIII are given explicit roman numerals in the section read; the rest are ordered here by birth date and flagged as inferred in each record's own note):
- **I. Lars Christensen Toft** (b.16.5.1771; d.15.8.1771, infant, 3 months). Faddere included Zidsel Larsdatter Toft (`@I2@`, his aunt).
- **II. Lauritz Christensen Toft** (b.8.7.1772; d.7.8.1847, "Ihjelslagen af en Hest," killed by a horse) married Mette Sørensdatter (d.12.12.1850, as his widow, age 78¾), gift 29. december (year not given, but their children's births place it o.1802). Three children: Christen Lauritzen Toft (1803), Karen Lauritzdatter Toft (1804), Søren Lauritzen Toft (1806).
- **III. Michel Christensen Toft** (b.16.3.1774). Confirmed 1792 alongside his sister Kirsten (IV.); faddere included Peder Boe (`@I1@`).
- **IV. Kirsten (Kierstine) Christensdatter Toft** (b.31.8.1775). Confirmed 1792, age 16 — the presiding priest's note was unflattering ("ringe af Begreb," poorly understood). Married Niels Jepsen 25.4.1800; their son Christen Nielsen was baptized 26.1.1800, before the wedding — Niels is recorded as "udlagt barnefader" (declared/acknowledged father). A second child, Inger Nielsdatter, followed in 1805.
- **[inferred V.] Maren Christensdatter Toft** (b.22.10.1777). Faddere included Peder Boe (`@I1@`).
- **[inferred VI.] Karen Christensdatter Toft** (b.20.4.1779; d.8.5.1853, unmarried, "Nyder Almisse," a poor-relief recipient).
- **[inferred VII.] Mads Christensen Toft** (b.30.1.1781).
- **VIII. Peder Christensen Toft** (b.11.2.1783), twin to a stillborn girl (unnamed, no record created). Almost certainly the "Peder Toft af Jørsby" buried 24.8.1806, age 23y24w — an age that matches this 1783 birth, not the 1785 birth below.
- **[a second, unlabeled] Peder Christensen Toft** (b.4.9.1785) — a second son with the identical name as VIII, born only 2 years later; the source gives no explanation, and if the 1806 burial record belongs to VIII (as the age suggests), the chronology of naming two living sons "Peder" is unclear. Not resolved here.
- **[inferred] Anders Christensen Toft** (b. o.1785; d. — the source's own text reads "22.6.1896," which is chronologically impossible given his stated age of 9 at death and o.1785 birth; read here as a "19" for "18" slip, i.e. 22.6.1796 — the same shape already logged as sub-pattern A in `family-tree/possible-duplicates.md`, not queued as a new instance since the intended reading is unambiguous).
- **[a second Michel, possibly = "XII."] Michel Christensen Toft** (b.21.1.1788) — the chapter's own Aneoversigt introduction separately mentions a son "Michel," numbered "XII.," whose parentage/legitimacy KEJ describes as uncertain ("en Inger Michelsdatter findes o.1788 gift med skoleholder Christen Lauritsen Toft... de fik 21.1. sønnen Michel") — plausibly this same birth, not confirmed. Also a name-reuse from III. above, again with no recorded death for the first Michel.
- **[inferred] Anna Christensdatter Toft** (b.3.8.1791; d.1.4.1794, infant). Faddere included her own brother, explicitly cited as "broder = II." — a direct confirmation that Lauritz (above) is indeed "II."

**b. Jens Lauritsen Toft** (b. o.1744) — a skrædder (tailor), later husmand med jord. Two marriages: an unnamed first wife (children born before 1790 belong to her) and Maren Justesdatter (2nd, trolovet 10.5.1789, viet 19.1.1790). From the first union: Christen Jensen (b. o.1774; d.15.5.1780, smallpox) and Kirsten Jensdatter (b.9.9.1776, twin to an unnamed stillborn sibling; faddere included Peder Boe — the source's own citation reads "kapitel 1 03," i.e. `@I4@`, generation "03," not `@I1@`). From the second union: Christen Jensen (b. o.1794, a name reused after the first Christen's 1780 death), Laurits Jensen (b. o.1798), and Birthe Jensdatter (b.13.7.1804).

**c. Maren Larsdatter Toft** (b. o.1745; d.3.12.1814, tentatively identified — the source marks the burial record with its own "?") married twice: Jens Sørensen Heede (1st, 20.2.1771, Jørsby kirke — "præstens annexbonde," the parish priest's tenant farmer) and, after being widowed ("efter lovligt skifte"), Poul Andersen (2nd, 1785, same tenancy). Five children from the first marriage — **Maren Jensdatter** (b.1771), **Maren Jensdatter** (b.1772, a second daughter of the same name, no death recorded for the first), **Margrethe Jensdatter** (b.1776, whose baptism was attended by "Kirsten Marie, tjenende Peder Lassen" — Peder Lassen (`@I7@`) is the same man at the center of the Kyssesekten story and Mads Pedersen's (`@I6@`) paternity), **Maren Jensdatter** (b.1782, a *third* daughter of this name, again no earlier death recorded), and **Ingeborg Jensdatter** (b.1783) — plus one from the second marriage, **Anders Poulsen** (b.1786), whose faddere included Christen Toft (`@I263@`, "= a.").

**d. Zidsel (Sidsel) Larsdatter Toft** — this is `@I2@`, already fully modeled from Chapter 1. This chapter adds: the exact time of daughter Anna's birth ("om Natten klokken 4"), and a considerably fuller kirkebog quotation on the Kyssesekten/Mads Pedersen paternity case than Chapter 1 carried, including the detail that Peder Lassen simultaneously **denied** paternity of two *other* named children (Zidsel's own, and one belonging to "Else Pedersdatter af Sundbye") — suggesting more paternity cases than this manuscript documents in full. The same passage also reveals Peder Lassen had yet another child, a son **Jens**, with his housekeeper **Maren Andersdatter** — a previously undocumented half-brother to Mads Pedersen, now added (`@I300@`, `@I301@`).

## Open Items — Data Quality

1. **Two sons both named "Peder Christensen Toft," born 1783 and 1785, to the same parents (Christen Lauritsen Toft and Inger Michelsdatter)** — no death is recorded for the first before the second's birth, unlike most other name-reuse cases in this chapter (which typically have an explicit, if terse, death record for the earlier bearer). A burial record ("Peder Toft af Jørsby," 24.8.1806, age 23y24w) matches the 1783 birth's age, not the 1785 birth's. Not resolved here — genuinely unclear whether this is a source transcription gap (a missing death record) or two brothers who, unusually, shared a name while both alive.
2. **A second instance of the same pattern, less certain:** "Michel Christensen Toft" appears twice for the same parents (1774 and 1788), and the chapter's own Aneoversigt introduction separately flags uncertainty about a "Michel," numbered "XII.," born under circumstances KEJ himself describes as unclear. Plausibly the 1788 birth and "XII." are the same person; not confirmed.
3. **Three separate daughters named "Maren Jensdatter," all children of Jens Sørensen Heede and Maren Larsdatter Toft** (b.1771, 1772, 1782) — no death is recorded for the first two in the section read. Given the volume of terse fragmentary kirkebog entries in this chapter, this reads as the same "incomplete record, not a real anomaly" character as items 1-2 above, not independently suspicious.
4. **A likely digit-transcription slip, corrected in `tree.ged` without a queue entry:** Anders Christensen Toft's death is given as "22.6.1896" in the source, which is chronologically impossible against his stated age (9) and o.1785 birth year — read as 22.6.1796. Same shape as `family-tree/possible-duplicates.md` sub-pattern A (a "19" swapped for an "18"), not logged as a new instance since the correct reading isn't in doubt.

None of the above are queued to `grandfather-review/queue.md` — per that queue's own scope (`projects/archive-digitization/TODO.md`'s "transcription vs. inference boundary" note), it's reserved for cases where KEJ himself is the best-placed person to resolve an ambiguity in *his own* research. These are all internal inconsistencies in centuries-old parish-register fragments that predate any research KEJ did himself, well beyond what a 2026 conversation with him could resolve — logged here as source-character documentation, not decisions for him to make.
